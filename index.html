THE GLOW GUIDE

Get the most
from your tan.

BEFORE

Prep your glow.

  • Exfoliate before your appointment.
  • Complete shaving before your tan.
  • Arrive with clean, dry skin.
  • Avoid lotions, oils and deodorant immediately beforehand.
  • Have loose, dark clothing ready for afterward.
AFTER

Make it last.

  • Follow your personalized rinse time.
  • Avoid sweating or water before your first rinse.
  • Pat your skin dry instead of rubbing.
  • Moisturize regularly after your first rinse.
  • Avoid aggressive exfoliation while maintaining your tan.
