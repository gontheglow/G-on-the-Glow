THE GLOW GUIDE

Prep well. Glow longer.

A little prep, a longer-lasting glow.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

Prep your glow.

  • Exfoliate gently about 24 hours before your appointment, especially on dry areas.
  • Shave or wax ahead of time to allow your skin to settle.
  • Arrive with clean, completely dry skin.
  • Avoid lotion, oil, deodorant, makeup, and perfume immediately before your tan.
  • Wear loose, dark clothing and comfortable shoes after your appointment.
  • Have a small space available for the mobile spray tanning setup.
AFTER YOUR APPOINTMENT

Keep your glow.

  • Follow your personalized rinse time. Rapid and traditional solutions have different development windows.
  • Avoid sweating, swimming, rain, and getting wet before your first rinse.
  • For your first rinse, use lukewarm water and avoid scrubbing. Follow the instructions for your specific solution.
  • After your first rinse, use a gentle, oil-free, non-exfoliating body wash.
  • Avoid harsh scrubs, exfoliating acids, and aggressive rubbing, which can make your tan fade faster.
  • Pat your skin dry and moisturize daily with a gentle, tan-friendly lotion.
  • Avoid long, hot baths to help maintain your color and encourage even fading.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Glow FAQ.

Is rapid spray tanning more expensive?

No! Rapid and traditional spray tan solutions are always the same price. Your appointment price depends only on your service area.

When should I rinse my spray tan?

Rapid solutions generally have a 1–5 hour rinse window, while traditional solutions typically have an 8–12 hour window. I'll give you the correct timing for your specific formula and desired shade.

Do I need to bring anything?

I'll bring my spray tanning equipment and complimentary supplies, including disposable coverage options, hair nets, nose plugs, and finishing powder. Just have a suitable space ready and loose clothing for afterward.

How do I pay?

Payment is collected after your appointment. I accept cash, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle. No online prepayment is required.

How long will my spray tan last?

Results vary depending on your skin, activities, and aftercare. Gentle washing, regular moisturizing, and avoiding harsh exfoliation can help maintain your glow.

Where do appointments take place?

G on the Glow is a mobile spray tanning service. I travel directly to clients in the Austin area, including Lakeway, Bee Cave, Westlake, Sunset Valley, Downtown Austin, Cedar Park, Round Rock, and Georgetown.
